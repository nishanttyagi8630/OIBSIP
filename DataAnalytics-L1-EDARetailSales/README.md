# EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Objective
Perform Exploratory Data Analysis on a retail (Superstore) sales dataset 
to uncover patterns, trends, and actionable business insights.

## Tech Stack
Python, pandas, matplotlib, seaborn, Google Colab

## Dataset
Superstore Sales Dataset (9800 rows, 18 columns) — sourced from Kaggle.

## Key Steps
- Data inspection (shape, dtypes, missing values)
- Data cleaning (handled missing Postal Code values, converted date columns)
- Time series analysis (monthly sales trend)
- Product & category-wise revenue analysis
- Correlation heatmap
- Region-wise sales analysis

## Key Insights
1. Sales peak in November-December (seasonal trend)
2. Technology category generates the highest revenue
3. West region performs best; South region has growth potential

## Business Recommendations
1. Plan inventory ahead of the holiday season
2. Increase marketing focus on Technology category
3. Launch targeted campaigns in the South region
